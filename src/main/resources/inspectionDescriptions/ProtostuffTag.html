Reports Protostuff @Tag mistakes that fail at runtime or have no effect:
  • A field without @Tag in a class where other fields have one. Once any field is tagged, Protostuff requires every serialized field to be tagged, including inherited ones.
  • Two serialized fields with the same number, including numbers used in a superclass.
  • A number below 1 or above 536870911 (229−1).
  • @Tag on a static, transient or @Exclude field, which Protostuff doesn't serialize.

Quick fixes tag the remaining fields, switch to the next free number, or remove a useless tag.
